# Monthly SEO check (about 30 minutes, free tools)

1. **Google Search Console** → Pages: any new "Not indexed" errors on public pages? Any private
   page (`/app`, `/portal`, `/admin`, `/sign`) showing up? (It shouldn't — tell Claude if it does.)
2. **Performance → Queries**: top queries, clicks and click-through rate. Note pages ranking 8–20 —
   they're the best candidates to improve.
3. **Core Web Vitals** report: all "Good"? (Targets: LCP under 2.5 s, INP under 200 ms, CLS under 0.1.)
4. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: same checks.
5. **Sitemap** submitted and read without errors (`https://<your site>/sitemap.xml`).
6. **New content**: anything published this month? Is it helpful, accurate and dated?
7. **Directories / Google Business Profile**: name, address and phone identical everywhere.
8. **Links**: affiliate/sponsored links use `rel="sponsored"`.
9. **No fake stars**: DeedWood's own pages never mark up their own reviews or ratings.
